Core Statistics

Actions

Every spell is tagged with its normal minimum slot or Free. Resource boxes sit on the action that spends them and stay synchronized with the overview above. Higher slots may be used to upcast when a spell says so.

Wild Shape Lodge

The form's printed HP is not used: you retain your own HP and gain the Moon Druid temporary HP shown above. The featured forms below use their printed attacks, movement, physical ability scores, and senses.

While transformed:
  • You retain your personality, memories, speech, creature type, HP, Hit Dice, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, languages, feats, class features, and your own skill/save proficiencies.
  • You normally can't cast spells, but transforming does not end Concentration. Circle spells are the exception: Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, Starry Wisp, Conjure Animals, Fount of Moonlight, and Mass Cure Wounds can be cast in Beast form once unlocked.
  • At Druid 6, Beast attacks may deal their normal type or Radiant, and you add Wisdom to Constitution saves in the form. War Caster still grants Advantage on concentration saves.
