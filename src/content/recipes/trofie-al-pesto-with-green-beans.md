<div class="step">

Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.

</div>

<div class="step">

Add the [[Medium potato, peeled and diced small]] and cook for 5 minutes.

</div>

<div class="step">

Add the [[Fresh green beans, trimmed and halved]] and [[Trofie pasta]] to the same pot. Cook until the pasta is al dente, about 10-12 minutes.

</div>

<div class="step">

Before draining, reserve about **1/2 cup** of the starchy pasta water. Drain the pasta, potato, and green beans together.

</div>

<div class="step">

Return everything to the pot off the heat. Add the [[Basil pesto, store-bought or homemade]] and [[Extra-virgin olive oil]], then toss until evenly coated. Add the reserved pasta water a splash at a time if needed to loosen the sauce.

</div>

<div class="step">

Season with the [[Fine salt]] and [[Cracked black pepper]]. Finish with the [[Parmesan cheese, finely grated]] and serve warm.

</div>

> **Traditional Ligurian method:** Cooking the trofie, potato, and green beans in the same pot lets the potato starch help bind the pesto. Genovese pesto is the classic choice.
